LESSON 1

The Zodiac

The limit of the manifest universe

CORE PRINCIPLE

The Zodiac is not a collection of personality types or mystical archetypes. It is a coordinate system — a 360° circle divided into 12 equal segments of 30° each. These segments represent the twelve signs, which form the structural framework for all astrological and tarot correspondences in this system.

The Zodiac defines the boundary of manifestation. Everything within the circle exists in time and space; everything outside it is unmanifest potential. This is not symbolism — it is geometry.

The Twelve Signs

Each sign occupies exactly 30° of the zodiacal circle. The sequence begins at the Vernal Equinox (0° Aries) and proceeds counter-clockwise through the traditional order.

KEY TAKEAWAY

The Zodiac is not symbolic. It is a structural grid. Every degree has a location. Every location has a rulership. Every rulership has a meaning. This system is calculable, not interpretive.

In the next lesson, we subdivide each 30° sign into three 10° segments called Decans, which form the foundation for the 36 small cards of the Tarot.
