The 36 Decans

The specific rulers of the Small Cards

CORE PRINCIPLE

Each of the 12 zodiacal signs is divided into three equal segments of 10 degrees each. These segments are called Decans (from the Greek deka, meaning "ten").

12 signs × 3 decans = 36 total decans. These 36 divisions form the structural foundation for the 36 small cards of the Tarot (Ace through 10 in each of the four suits).

Each decan has a specific planetary ruler that determines its essential nature. This is not arbitrary attribution — it follows the ancient Chaldean order of planetary velocities.

The Chaldean Order

The seven classical planets are arranged by their apparent speed through the zodiac, from slowest to fastest. This sequence determines decan rulership.

KEY TAKEAWAY

The 36 decans are not symbolic interpretations. They are precise 10° segments of the zodiacal circle, each governed by a specific planet following the unchanging Chaldean order.

Every small card in the Tarot deck is the direct expression of its corresponding decan. The card's meaning is determined by: Planet + Sign + Position.
