LESSON 6

Aspects in Layouts

Trines, squares, and oppositions within a spread

CORE PRINCIPLE

When cards appear together in a spread, they do not exist in isolation. Their zodiacal positions create geometric relationships called aspects — the same angular patterns used in astrological chart interpretation.

These aspects reveal how cards interact: whether they flow harmoniously (trine), create tension (square), or oppose each other (opposition). This is not metaphor — it is spatial geometry on the zodiacal wheel.

Recognizing aspects transforms spread reading from isolated card meanings into dynamic relational networks.

KEY TAKEAWAY

Aspects are not interpretive associations. They are geometric facts derived from zodiacal positions. If two cards occupy signs that are 120° apart, they form a trine — this is calculable, not symbolic.

In a spread, always check for aspects. They reveal the hidden geometry of the reading — which energies flow, which clash, which mirror.
