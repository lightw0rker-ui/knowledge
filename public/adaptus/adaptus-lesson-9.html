CONFLICT ARBITRATION

Tree-Based Resolution

Contradictions in a reading are not errors; they are Jurisdictional Disputes.
We resolve them by mapping each card to the Tree of Life. The card with the higher Authority (Lower Index Number) executes its command.

The Permission Hierarchy

Imagine the Tree of Life as a corporate or military hierarchy.

A card in Kether (1) is a CEO directive. A card in Malkuth (10) is a janitorial schedule. If the CEO says "Go" and the Schedule says "Stop", the CEO wins.

Resolution Logic

IF Card A is Higher on the Tree (Lower Number) THEN Card A is the Cause and Card B is merely a Symptom.

Example: Magician (1) vs 10 of Wands (10).
The Oppression (10) is real, but the Will (1) has the authority to change it. The outcome is defined by the Magician.
