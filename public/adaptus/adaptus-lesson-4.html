COMBINATORIAL LOGIC

Pairs & Triplicities

A single symbol is a variable. Two symbols are a function. Three symbols are a statement.
We do not read isolated meanings. We read the New Reality generated by collision.

I. Binary Collision (The Pair)

INPUT A
Emperor
AUTHORITY
INPUT B
The Tower
DISRUPTION

OUTPUT SYNTHESIS

"The Overthrow of a Tyrant"

Neither card means "Overthrow" alone. The event is generated only by the intersection of Authority and Disaster.

II. Linear Syntax (The Triad)

When three cards appear in sequence, they follow a sentence structure: Subject + Verb + Object.

NARRATIVE PARSER
SUBJECT
Knight Cups
"The Romantic"
The person or actor initiating the event.
VERB
8 of Wands
"Rushes"
The action, speed, or modifying force.
OBJECT
The Devil
"Into Toxicity"
The result, destination, or recipient of action.
COMPILED OUTPUT:

"A romantic rushes headlong into a toxic situation."

Protocol: "The Glance"

Do not parse every triad. Processing power is limited.
1. Locate the Significator. Read the triad centered on them.
2. Locate Major Arcana. Read triads centered on Majors.
Treat the rest as noise unless specifically counted to.
