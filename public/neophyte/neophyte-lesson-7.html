01. Sephiroth as Functions

Novices treat the Sephiroth (nodes on the Tree of Life) as gods. Adepts treat them as Functions.

A Function has two properties:
1. Constraint (What it limits).
2. Affordance (What it enables).

Fate is simply the default momentum of the system—inertia. Will is the deliberate application of force to alter that momentum. Without Will, you are a satellite crashing into the planet.
