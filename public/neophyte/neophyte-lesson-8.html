Projection & Transference
The Smudged Mirror

"The wire does not judge the signal. It simply carries the current."

01. Operational Coldness

Projection occurs when the reader's ego fills the gaps in the symbols. You see "Death" and feel your fear of change, rather than the client's reality.

Transference occurs when the client projects authority onto you. They want a parent, a judge, or a savior.

To operate safely, you must cultivate Operational Coldness. You are not a "helper." You are a telephone wire. The wire is neutral. It conveys "I love you" and "I hate you" with equal fidelity. It does not add its own opinion to the message.

02. Protocol: Polishing the Mirror

The mirror is clouded by your own noise. Click to Scrub the surface until the image is clear.
