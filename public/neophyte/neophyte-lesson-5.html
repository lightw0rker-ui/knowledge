The Cascade of Creation

"From the spark to the flame, from the design to the stone."

01. Abstraction Layers

Qabalah teaches that reality does not happen all at once. It descends through four layers of density, like a lightning flash grounding into the earth. We call these The Four Worlds.

Understand them as "Resolutions":
1. Atziluth (Archetype): The raw impulse. Low resolution, high energy.
2. Briah (Blueprint): The concept or pattern. Fluid and planning.
3. Yetzirah (Formation): The mechanics and wireframes. Conflict and specificity.
4. Assiah (Manifestation): The physical object. High resolution, low energy (frozen).

The Trap: Most mental anguish comes from confusing Yetzirah (the mental story) with Assiah (the physical fact).
