01. Sanitizing the Workspace

The Lesser Banishing Ritual of the Pentagram (LBRP) is often misunderstood as "ghost busting." In the Neophyte grade, we view it as Mental Hygiene. It is a "Hard Reset" for the consciousness.

Just as a computer needs to clear its RAM before running a heavy application, the Magician must clear "cognitive debris"—daily stress, petty thoughts, and emotional noise—before engaging with the Tarot.

The ritual inscribes a "Magic Circle" (a boundary of attention) and populates it with four "Archangels" (elemental archetypes) to balance the psyche.
