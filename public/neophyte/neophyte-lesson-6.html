01. The Trap of Labels

A keyword (e.g., "Death means Change") is a useful lie for beginners. It provides a handle to grasp the archetype. But if you hold the handle too long, you never touch the blade.

This is Idolatry: worshipping the label instead of the force. A reader who sees the Ten of Swords and thinks "Betrayal" without looking at the image is not reading Tarot; they are reading a flashcard.

To advance, you must become an Iconoclast. You must shatter the rigid keyword to release the living energy trapped inside.
