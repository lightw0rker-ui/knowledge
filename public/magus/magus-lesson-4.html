The Major Arcana
as Stargates

The Minor Arcana are the domain of Angels (Shem HaMephorash). The Major Arcana are the domain of Forces. Specifically, the seven "Planetary" trumps act as direct interfaces to the celestial spheres.

To invoke a planet, you do not merely contemplate the card. You use the Hexagram of the Planet to unlock the card's frequency. This turns the image from a picture into a portal.

Direct Correspondence

  • The Magician = Mercury (Communication, Tech)
  • The Empress = Venus (Desire, Nature)
  • The Tower = Mars (Severity, Deconstruction)
  • The Sun = Sol (Radiance, Success)
  • The Wheel = Jupiter (Expansion, Luck)
  • The Universe = Saturn (Structure, Binding)
  • High Priestess = Luna (Intuition, Flux)

The Invocation Sequence

RITUAL
01. ALIGNMENT

Wait for the correct Planetary Hour. You cannot effectively invoke the Sun at midnight on a Tuesday. Timing reduces resistance.

02. TRACING

Draw the Invoking Hexagram over the card in the air. Visualize it in the planet's color (e.g., Golden-Yellow for Sol).

03. CHARGE

Vibrate the Godname (e.g., YHVH Eloah Ve-Daath for Sol). The card should appear to "flash" with light.

04. LICENSE

When the operation is done, give the License to Depart. Do not leave planetary portals open in your workspace.
