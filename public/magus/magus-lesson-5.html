The Table as Microcosm

A Divination Spread is passive; it asks, "What will happen?" A Ritual Spread is active; it commands, "Let this happen."

In this grade, the spread is a Circuit Board. You do not shuffle randomly. You deliberately place cards to create a flow of energy. The positions (East, South, Center) act as stations of power, and the cards act as the components (Resistors, Capacitors, Sources).

Engineering Analogy

"If the Tarot deck is the code, the Ritual Spread is the hardware architecture. A Triangle focuses. A Circle contains. A Cross stabilizes."
