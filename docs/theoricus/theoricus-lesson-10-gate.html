LESSON 10 — FINAL

The Gate

Calculating the decan and ruler of any given date

FINAL LESSON

This is the practical culmination of the Theoricus grade. You have learned the zodiacal wheel, the 36 decans, planetary rulerships, court correspondences, elemental thrones, aspects, timing, and synthesis.

Now you will demonstrate operational fluency: given any calendar date, you must calculate which decan the Sun occupies, which planet rules that decan, and which Tarot card corresponds to it.

This skill is called The Gate because it opens the passage to the next level. Complete the challenge successfully to unlock LEVEL IV — PRACTICUS.

The Calculation Method

A four-step process to derive decan, ruler, and card from any date.

1️⃣
Identify the Sign
Determine which zodiacal sign the Sun occupies on the given date. Use the solar calendar: Aries (Mar 21–Apr 19), Taurus (Apr 20–May 20), etc.
2️⃣
Determine the Decan
Each sign has three decans of ~10 days each. Days 1–10 = Decan 1, Days 11–20 = Decan 2, Days 21–30 = Decan 3.
3️⃣
Find the Ruler
Apply the Chaldean order starting with Mars in Aries Decan 1. Count forward through the planetary sequence: Mars, Sun, Venus, Mercury, Moon, Saturn, Jupiter.
4️⃣
Identify the Card
Match the decan to its Tarot card. Fire signs → Wands (2–10), Water → Cups, Air → Swords, Earth → Pentacles. The first decan of each element = 2, continuing to 10 at the final decan.
