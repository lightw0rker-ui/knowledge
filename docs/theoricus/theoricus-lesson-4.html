LESSON 4

The 12 Courts

Zodiacal segments (Knight, Queen, King)

CORE PRINCIPLE

The Court Cards represent the twelve zodiacal signs themselves, not individual personalities or archetypes. Each sign is expressed through three hierarchical ranks: Knight, Queen, and King.

These three ranks correspond to the three decans within each sign, creating a precise mapping: 12 signs × 3 ranks = 36 Court positions. However, the traditional Tarot deck contains only 16 Court cards (4 suits × 4 ranks).

The resolution: Knights, Queens, and Kings govern the zodiacal signs directly. The fourth rank (Pages/Princesses) operates on a different axis entirely — they represent the elemental thrones, not zodiacal positions.

EXAMPLE ANALYSIS

Knight of Wands vs. King of Wands

Knight of Wands
Aries 0° – 10° (Decan 1)
Pure initiating fire. The beginning of Aries, ruled by Mars. Maximum yang energy. This is the Knight as pure impulse — direct, aggressive, pioneering action without hesitation or reflection. The first spark.
King of Wands
Aries 20° – 30° (Decan 3)
Established fire. The end of Aries, ruled by Venus. The initial impulse now stabilized and directed toward creation. This is the Knight matured — still fiery, but with purpose, control, and sovereign authority. The flame sustained.

The same sign (Aries), the same element (Fire), but different phases of manifestation. The Knight begins. The Queen deepens. The King completes.

KEY TAKEAWAY

Court Cards are not personality types. They are precise zodiacal positions expressed through hierarchical rank.

Knight = First decan (0°–10°)
Queen = Second decan (10°–20°)
King = Third decan (20°–30°)

This leaves one rank unexplained: the Page (or Princess). In the next lesson, we discover that Pages operate on an entirely different axis — they represent the four elemental thrones themselves, not zodiacal subdivisions.
