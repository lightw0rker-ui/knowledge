Level III — Theoricus

The Clockwork of the Heavens
Astrology as Structure for the Minor Arcana

Zodiac (12) Decans (36) Planets (7) Card Coordinates

Theoricus establishes astrological architecture as the coordinate system for the Minor Arcana. The goal is precision: the 36 Small Cards (2–10) become specific locations in the Zodiac via the 36 Decans.

This is not “vibes.” It is a clockwork: sign, decan, ruler, and aspectual geometry. The student learns to compute meaning from structure: Planet + Sign + Number ? operational reading.
