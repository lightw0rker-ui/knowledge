The 72-Fold Name
The Grid of Angels

The Tarot's Minor Arcana are not merely scenes of daily life. They are the administration grid of the Shem HaMephorash—the 72 Names of God extracted from three verses in the Book of Exodus.

Each Small Card (2 through 10) covers 10 degrees of the Zodiac. Since each Angel governs 5 degrees (a "Quinance"), every Small Card houses two Angels.
