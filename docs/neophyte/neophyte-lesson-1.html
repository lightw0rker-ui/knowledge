01. The Interface Model

We reject the notion of Tarot as "fortune telling." Instead, we adopt the Interface Model. The deck is a database of 78 archetypal images. You are the operator. To use the system safely, you must define your inputs and constraints before you engage.

1. Input The Random Variable
(The Card)
2. Operator The Cognitive Action
(Describe, Compare)
3. Constraint The Safety Limit
(Timebox, Topic)

Forbidden Operations

  • Prediction: Declaring future facts ("You will meet a tall stranger").
  • Coercion: Prescribing specific actions ("You must leave your job").
  • Externalization: Attributing your intuition to spirits/demons.
