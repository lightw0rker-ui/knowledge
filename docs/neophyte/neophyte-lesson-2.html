01. Salience & Inflation

Salience is the "voltage" of a symbol—how important it feels to you. When you open a Tarot deck, you are deliberately increasing salience. You are telling your brain: "Look here. This matters."

The danger is Inflation. This occurs when the pattern-matching engine of the brain overheats, finding connections where there are none, leading to paranoia, obsession, or "Main Character Syndrome."

We prevent this not by avoiding the cards, but by installing Circuit Breakers (Silence and Stop Rules).
