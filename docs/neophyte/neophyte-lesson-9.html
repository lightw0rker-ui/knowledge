Geometry vs. Chronology

"The Tarot is not a story you read. It is a machine you build."

01. Chronos vs. Kairos

Beginners love the "Fool's Journey" because it is a story. It has a beginning (Fool), a middle (Tower), and an end (World). This is Chronos (Linear Time).

But the Universe does not work in lines; it works in networks. The Magician (I) does not "meet" The High Priestess (II). They generate each other simultaneously, like the positive and negative poles of a battery. This is Kairos (Deep Structure).

To master the Tarot, you must stop asking "What happens next?" and start asking "How does the circuit flow?"

03. Narrative Breaker

Identify the flaw in the reading. Distinguish Story Logic from Circuit Logic.
