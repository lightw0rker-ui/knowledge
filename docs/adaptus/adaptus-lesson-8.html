Saturnian Blockage

Not every silence is empty. Some silence is a Structural Refusal.
When the deck refuses to compute, it is not a glitch. It is a protective protocol known as the Saturnian Lock.

Diagnostic Criteria

A Blockage is distinct from a "bad reading." It is characterized by recursion, friction, and a lack of synthesis. The deck is effectively saying: "Input Invalid or Access Denied."

  • RECURSION LOOP The same symbol appears repeatedly but refuses to form a narrative chain.
  • THERMAL RUNAWAY The Querent's anxiety escalates, but the data clarity decreases.
  • ETHICAL FIREWALL The question violates operational parameters (e.g., spying, harm).

ACCESS DENIED

SATURNIAN PROTOCOL ACTIVE

"The current query structure cannot be resolved within the present constraints."

Recovery Procedures

TROUBLESHOOTING
PROTOCOL 01

Immediate Halt

Stop the reading. Do not force a narrative. A forced answer is hallucinated data. Acknowledge the silence as a valid answer.

PROTOCOL 02

Reframe Scope

The question may be malformed. Shift from "What will happen?" (Prediction) to "What is the next step?" (Procedural).

PROTOCOL 03

Total Abort

If the blockage persists or ethical lines are crossed, terminate the session. Do not re-attempt for 24 hours.

Practice: The Diagnostic Log

When a lock occurs, document it. This preserves the integrity of the system.
