SYSTEM GRAMMAR

Elemental Dignities

In standard reading, cards are definitions. In Adeptus reading, cards are forces.
A force cannot act in a vacuum. It is strengthened, neutralized, or destroyed by its environment.

The Syntax of Power

Imagine the Card "Ace of Wands" (Pure Fire/Action).

  • Placed in FIRE (e.g., Summer): Explosive Action.
  • Placed in WATER (e.g., Ocean): Extinguished / Steam.
  • Placed in EARTH (e.g., Cave): Slow Heat / Sustained.

This system determines if an event actually happens (Action) or if it is merely a wish that fails to manifest (Extinguished).

FIRE + FIRE = POWER
FIRE + WATER = NULL
