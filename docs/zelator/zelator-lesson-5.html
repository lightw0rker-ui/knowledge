Formula Logic

Stability vs. Instability

Even numbers (2, 4, 6, 8) are stable. They represent balance and structure.
Odd numbers (3, 5, 7, 9) are unstable. They represent movement and conflict.

RULE: A 4 is always a "pause" or "room." A 5 is always a "breaking" or "motion."

Elemental Physics

Resistance Calculation

Fire and Air are active/light. Earth and Water are passive/heavy.
When you put a heavy number (10) on a light suit (Wands), the energy is "oppressed." (10 of Wands = Oppression). When you put a light number (Ace) on a heavy suit, it is just a seed.
